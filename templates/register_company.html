Understanding BridgeIT Roles

BridgeIT has three distinct user roles:

  • Students: Use the app platform for career guidance, courses, and job opportunities
  • Guides: Provide mentorship, create content, and help students (requires admin approval)
  • Companies: Post jobs, exams, and bootcamps for students (requires admin approval)

Note: Admins manage the platform and approve Guide/Company registrations.
